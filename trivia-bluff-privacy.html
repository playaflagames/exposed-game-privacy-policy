Trivia Bluff — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Trivia Bluff is a pass-the-phone party game made by AFLA Games. This policy is short because the app collects nothing.

The short version
  • We collect no data. Not from you, not from your kids, not from anyone.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app never connects to our servers — we don't have any.
  • Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To make the game work, Trivia Bluff saves a few things locally on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Player names and characters from your last game (so Quick Play can start the same group again)
  • Which questions you've already seen (so you don't get repeats)
  • Your settings (sound, music)
  • Whether you've unlocked the full game

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time by deleting the app. "Forget Seen Questions" in Settings also clears the question history.

In-app purchase

Trivia Bluff offers one optional, one-time purchase ("Full Game"). Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Sharing

If you use Share on the winner screen, your device's standard share sheet opens and you choose where the image goes. We don't see or store anything you share.

Children

Trivia Bluff is designed for families to play together, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. There are no chats, no links out to the web, and no ads.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal information, and we use them only to fix bugs.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Common questions

How many players?

2 to 8, on one phone or iPad. With exactly 2 players the game switches to Duel: one sets a number, the other calls Over or Under.

What's The Plant?

An optional twist you switch on in setup (4+ players). One guesser is secretly handed a fake number and has to sell it. The Judge scores for spotting them.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. Family mode is on by default: easy questions and kid-safe topics only.

What does the Full Game purchase include?

I'm seeing questions I've played before.
